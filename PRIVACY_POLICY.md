# Verifai – Privacy Policy

*Last updated: May 2026*

Verifai does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal 
data or user information of any kind.

All image analysis is performed entirely within your local browser 
using a bundled on-device model. Images you upload or select are 
never sent to any external server or third party.

No analytics, tracking, or telemetry of any kind is used.
